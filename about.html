Store Story

Founded 2020

NANA OPTOM was founded in 2020 by Shafeeva Anastasia Valeryevna. The store is located in Shymkent and specializes in wholesale cosmetics. It offers different cosmetics and beauty products to customers. NANA OPTOM also provides delivery across Kazakhstan.

Our Products

NANA OPTOM offers cosmetics from different brands, including luxury and affordable products. Customers can find skincare and makeup products at wholesale prices. For example, the store offers products from brands such as Round Lab, Sen Sulu, Celimax, and other cosmetic brands. The assortment includes skincare, face, eye, and lip products for different needs and budgets. cosmetics skincare products wholesale cosmetics
